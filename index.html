About

Bringing the lights back on, at the edge of the rainforest.

KASURIA is a student-led community outreach project under UTP’s MPU4 programme. We are working with the Orang Asli community at Sungai Tiang, Royal Belum, Perak, to repair and renew a solar power system that has stopped working. Our site visit is complete, and we are now looking for sponsors to make the repairs possible.

01

Community first

Every step follows JAKOA protocols and is shaped with the community, not for them.

02

Engineered properly

Chemical engineering students apply energy, safety and systems thinking to a real, remote site.

03

Built to last

Training and handover so the system keeps running long after the install team leaves.

What We Found

Our site visit, in photos

We travelled to Sungai Tiang to see the existing solar system first-hand. The panels and equipment are no longer working. Our plan is to repair what can be saved, replace the battery, and find parts that are viable and affordable to maintain.

1

Repair

Check which panels, wiring and the inverter can be restored safely.

2

Replace the battery

The battery needs replacing. We are researching options that suit the site.

3

Find what is viable

Choose parts the community can look after and replace in future.
